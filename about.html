About Me

The story behind the pixels

Hi, I'm Satwik

I'm a passionate Video Editor, Motion Designer, and inspiring VFX Artist currently pursuing my BSc in Animation at AAFT University, Noida. With over a year of professional freelance experience and a creative drive that never stops, I transform raw footage into compelling visual narratives.

My journey began in 2025 when I started freelancing as a video editor, completing over 50 projects for clients worldwide. This hands-on experience taught me the importance of storytelling, attention to detail, and meeting deadlines in the fast-paced world of content creation.

Today, I continue to push creative boundaries — whether it's through cinematic color grading, dynamic motion graphics, or breathtaking visual effects. Every project is an opportunity to create something memorable.
